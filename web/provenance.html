Provenance

Provenance registry. EVERY claim in the atlas must reference a source entry. Trust tiers rank source types - higher tier wins on conflict. Tiers are ordered: 1 highest to 5 lowest.

tier 1

Lab test (COA)

Certificates of analysis from licensed/accredited labs - cannabinoid and terpene quantification, contamination screens.

Overriding claimed THC ranges with measured potency.

tier 2

Cup result

Published results from recognized competitions (High Times Cannabis Cup, Emerald Cup, etc.).

Award claims on strain entries.

tier 3

Breeder claim

Statements from the original breeder / seed producer (catalog copy, lineage notes, lab tests commissioned by breeder).

Lineage and flowering-time claims - always flag as breeder-asserted.

tier 4

Community report

Grower reports, forum archives, seedbank reviews, phenotype write-ups. Aggregated signal, individual noise.

Phenotype variation ranges, cultivation notes.

tier 5

Academic

Peer-reviewed chemistry/phytochemistry literature and compound databases (PubChem etc.). Strong for compounds, weak for consumer claims.

Terpene boiling points, flavonoid identification.
